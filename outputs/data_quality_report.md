# Data-quality report

Generated by `src/clean.py`. Datasets: National Power Portal (npp.gov.in) and the organiser CSV only; no assumed values.

- Organiser CSV: 635 rows, 34 states/UTs, calendar years 2006-2024 (treated as FY2007-FY2025, i.e. CSV year Y = fiscal year Y-(Y+1)).
- Columns ['Installed - Bio-Mass Power', 'Installed - Small Hydro Power', 'Installed - Solar Power', 'Installed - Wind Power', 'Installed - Waste to Energy'] are INSTALLED CAPACITY (MW) -> never used for the level of generation or any comparison; only their composition (shares) apportions NPP's RES total among solar / wind / other and regions.
- 2024 capacity rows identical to 2023 (carry-forward): 19 of 34 -> 2024 rows flagged PROVISIONAL.
- CSV 'Zone' differs from the official CEA 5-region grid mapping for: Andaman And Nicobar Islands (South->ER), Chhattisgarh (Central->WR), Madhya Pradesh (Central->WR), Rajasthan (West->NR), Sikkim (North-East->ER). 'Central' is not a grid region. Remapped to NR/WR/SR/ER/NER.
- Chandigarh: CSV starts 2013; population backcast to 2006 at its own 1.45%/yr (CSV 2013-2016).
- Delhi: CSV starts 2009; population backcast to 2006 at its own 2.26%/yr (CSV 2009-2012).
- Telangana 2013 row missing -> log-linear interpolation (this row causes the dip in the raw CSV all-India population for 2013: 1.206 bn vs 1.225 bn in 2012).
- GSDP base break at 2011 spliced with the CSV's own pre-2011 growth rates (28 states) or CSV aggregate growth (constant/imputed series: Andaman And Nicobar Islands, Chandigarh, Delhi, Jammu And Kashmir, Lakshadweep, Telangana).
- GSDP 2022-2024 show imputation (linear 2021->2023 increments, fractional values); 2024 total = 2023 x 0.998 (carry-forward) -> 2024 set to 2023 x (1 + 3.95%) (the CSV's own 2023 growth). Flagged PROVISIONAL.
- Coverage: Ladakh and Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu are not in the CSV (J&K rows include Ladakh before 2019); together <0.1% of population. No external data is added to fill them.
- National target = NPP all-India utility generation (hydro + thermal + RES + nuclear, excl. Bhutan import), FY2007-FY2024 from the NPP history endpoint - every year an NPP actual. NPP publishes no RES after FY2024, so FY2024 is the base year; FY2025-FY2026 conventional actuals (NPP sub-report 16) are kept for out-of-sample validation.
- Estimation sample FY2007-FY2024 (18 years): generation 671 -> 1,698 TWh (5.6%/yr); CSV population 1.121 -> 1.387 bn; CSV real GSDP 5.9%/yr.
- NPP INTERNAL CONSISTENCY (48 overlapping FY x fuel comparisons across four NPP products): max |difference| 0.13%, median 0.000% -> PASS vs +/-2% bar. Gas (small, 1.3% max) differs because DGR17 counts liquid fuel as 'diesel'.
- FY2024 regional mix: coal, gas, hydro, nuclear = NPP sub-report 16 regional actuals; RES = NPP national actual (189.5 TWh) apportioned by the CSV RE-fleet composition (solar 63% / wind 27% / other 10%; regional shares of each). NPP publishes no solar/wind or regional RES split.
